Security settings open in the existing account service. Password options depend on your sign-in method.

Delete account

This permanently removes your Mossvale sign-in account and all characters, progress, inventory, and social connections in Europe and North America. Reports you submitted, or that concern your account, are removed too.

You will lose access to game purchases attached to these characters. Deletion does not automatically request a refund. Purchase, receipt and refund records remain for payment verification and recovery, along with a limited account-deletion record. Public blockchain transactions cannot be erased.

Resolve any MOSS store payment or auction reservation that is still settling before deletion. Read the deletion details.
