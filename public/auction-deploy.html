Auction settlement setup

Deploy the settlement contract for MOSS or ETH auctions with your wallet. The wallet pays the network deployment fee. This page never asks for a private key.

MOSS on Robinhood Chain: 0x6742883Eef788E2424CE5a1b0d4303144AaEc0a5 Use the public address of the signing key stored in your server’s MOSS_AUCTION_AUTHORITY_KEY (MOSS) or AUCTION_AUTHORITY_KEY (ETH) secret. This authority is permanent for this contract.
Use the existing TREASURE_TREASURY_CONTRACT address. The treasury address is permanent for this contract.

Every MOSS sale deducts a 5% fee: 1.25% of the purchase price goes to the treasury, 1.25% to the dev team, and 2.5% to burning before referral rewards. A 100 MOSS sale pays the seller 95 MOSS, burns 2.5 MOSS, and sends 1.25 MOSS each to treasury and dev team. Qualified referral milestones of 1, 10, 25, 50 and 100 award 0.10%, 0.25%, 0.50%, 0.75% and 1.00% of the purchase price to the inviter, deducted from burning. At 100 qualified referrals a 100 MOSS sale burns 1.5 MOSS and credits 1 MOSS to the inviter. The buyer pays the listed price. Burning reduces the token’s total supply. Rewards use the existing proceeds withdrawal; fee, recipient shares and rewards round down to the smallest token unit, with the remainder burned. This version requires a new contract deployment and configuration. Existing contracts and pending purchases keep their original terms.

Fixed dev team recipient: 0x6D96b833C760774175Cc6E2c4F7424122EA3798f. This address cannot be changed for this contract.

Existing game listings can carry over without relisting. Before activating a replacement, release support for settling original payments and withdrawing old seller balances, or finish all pending purchases first and preserve the original withdrawal address.
