# Player feedback implementation

Implemented from the supplied Mossvale feedback document, including the larger additions and subsequent revisions requested by the user.

| Feedback | Implemented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Talent choices and combat identity | 20 points at level 60, one-time refund of legacy allocations, out-of-combat resets, school and playstyle bonuses, and two/four-piece gear effects. Mage spells include fire damage over time, frost shatter combinations and arcane executes. |
| Cast pacing and combat information | Repeatable core hard casts have no extra cooldown after completion. Major abilities retain their cooldowns. Own damage-over-time effects show expiry timers on enemies and the target frame; out-of-range spells display a red cue. |
| Distinct places and discoveries | Slimefen and Sugarbloom Orchard, two hidden curios, and seven named resource sites with shared claims and faster replenishment. Caves, a quarry and a mine use authoritative collision. |
| Dreams | Resting in a reachable bed restores health and has a 35% chance of a private pleasant dream or nightmare. Explore for keepsakes for three minutes, or wake early at a portal. Rest has a saved ten-minute interval. Rewards survive waking; death and disconnect return the character safely. |
| Fishing | A fourth gathering profession with four fish tiers, shoreline shoals, a fishing rod and animation, shared resource claims, bag checks, edible catches and sale values. |
| Objective navigation | Ground arrows follow safe route segments, with a marker over nearby objectives. Checkpoints advance within five meters or after you pass them toward the next point, provided the next segment is clear. Missing an intermediate marker no longer sends you back. Retained waypoints rebuild their route from the arrival position after Zeppelin travel. The Controls setting disables guidance. |
| Appearance | Native arbitrary-color pickers, highlights, individual head/body/leg/shoe/back dyes, sixteen hairstyles, and fox tails colored from skin. Catfolk, Dogfolk and Lizardfolk bring the roster to nine races. |
| Authored models | Detailed Blender sources, optimized game exports and inspection galleries for the race/customization libraries and the new world props. Existing Mossvale art direction and instanced world rendering are preserved. |

The wiki documents the new mechanics. New races and appearance options are available without adding a payment requirement.

## Verification

`npm run check:feedback` exercises the new navigation/range/timer logic and real-server combat, dream and fishing flows. Existing progression, casting, talents, raid balance, gear, appearance persistence, character animation, onboarding, gathering, navigation and wiki checks cover the affected systems. `npm run build` checks TypeScript and builds the game.

Visual checks use an isolated local realm with synthetic characters. Production player data is not used. This implementation does not deploy the game or publish the wiki.
