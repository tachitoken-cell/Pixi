Mossvale privacy notice

Last updated 27 September 2026

This notice describes information used by the Mossvale game, website, account service and mobile app. For privacy questions, contact support@mossvale.world.

Information we useServices involvedStorageAdvertising cookiesAccount deletionYour choices

Information we use

  • Account and sign-in information. The account service processes your account identifier, username, email when provided, credentials for your chosen sign-in method, and sign-in sessions. The game uses the verified account identifier to associate your characters with you.
  • Gameplay information. Character names and appearance, realm activity, progression, inventory, settings, achievements, friends, ignored players, trades and auction activity are used to run the game and save your adventure.
  • Communications and reports. Messages are delivered to their selected game audience. Player reports can include a message excerpt, character details, report reason and moderator decision. Support emails contain the information you choose to send.
  • Fair-play review. During active, focused play, the game sends coarse summaries of browser window dimensions, click timing and counts of clicks, key presses, touch presses, drags and resizes. The game also exchanges short-lived runtime checks and reports whether the browser declares automation control. The server records aggregate check results, explicit scripting-client indicators and repeated accepted gameplay routes. These observations support review of suspicious gameplay and may be stored with a security report and moderator decision; raw client headers and runtime challenge values are not stored in reports. They do not include typed content, key values, raw pointer coordinates or the page elements you used. Browser summaries alone are not proof of botting.
  • Purchase information. Where purchases are available, we process product and order identifiers, purchase verification results, character delivery records, and refund or recovery status. Apple and Google handle their payment checkout; Mossvale does not receive your full payment-card details from that checkout.
  • Wallet information. If you choose wallet sign-in or web wallet features, we process the public wallet address and signed ownership proof. Web MOSS payments also use public transaction data. Mossvale does not ask for a wallet private key or recovery phrase.
  • Optional mobile notifications. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token, your notification choices, your selected realm and recent account activity to send world-event alerts and invitations. If you also enable return reminders, the first can arrive after 24 hours away, then at most once per day until you return. Expo, Apple and Google process the token and notification content to deliver alerts; invitations may include the inviting character’s name. Manage each category in Settings → Notifications or disable notifications in device settings. Choices remain saved on this device when you sign out. Signing out requests removal of the subscription; if offline, removal retries when the app reconnects. Subscriptions expire after 90 days without refresh.
  • Connection and device information. Requests expose technical information such as IP address, browser or app information and connection errors to the services delivering the game. This information supports delivery, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. We also estimate connection country for aggregate player statistics using local IP-to-country data or country information supplied by our trusted network edge. This country is an approximate network location and can reflect a VPN. The statistics collector does not store IP addresses or send them to an external lookup service; it keeps country codes and private hashed account tokens for cross-realm deduplication, removing those records during scheduled cleanup after 31 days without activity. The public dashboard shows country totals and unknown coverage, never these account tokens.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to authenticate accounts, maintain saved progress, operate multiplayer features, verify and deliver purchases, address support requests, and enforce community rules. Character names, appearance, auction listings and messages are visible to the players who participate in the relevant game feature. A whisper is delivered to its participants; a reported excerpt can be reviewed by game masters.

Only send information in game chat that you are comfortable sharing with its audience. Other players may keep copies or screenshots outside Mossvale’s control.

Services involved

Mossvale uses its Keycloak account service for sign-in, hosting and database services for the game, and Cloudflare for network delivery. The game has Europe and North America realms; infrastructure can process requests outside your country.

Native wallet sign-in opens the account sign-in page inside your chosen MetaMask, Phantom, OKX or Trust Wallet browser. The wallet processes your connection and signing requests directly. Older app builds may use their wallet provider’s connection services to relay encrypted requests; optional connector analytics are disabled in those builds. Your wallet shows the identity message for your approval; connecting does not authorize a payment. If you make an enabled mobile purchase, Apple’s App Store or Google Play processes the store transaction and provides verification information. Their own privacy notices apply to their services.

Public blockchain networks and RPC services process wallet-related requests and transactions when those web features are used. Public transaction history is independently accessible. The game also loads its current display fonts from Google Fonts; these account, support and privacy pages use device fonts and do not request external fonts.

Google and Apple sign-in

If you choose an available Google or Apple sign-in option, that provider authenticates you and shares an account identifier and the profile information you approve, such as an email address. Apple may provide a private relay email address. Mossvale does not receive your Google or Apple password. Your provider link is stored by the account service so you can return to the same Mossvale account.

The account service retains Apple authorization tokens on the server to revoke the connection when you unlink Apple or delete your Mossvale account. Deleting Mossvale does not delete your Google or Apple account. You can also review connected apps through your Google account or your Apple account.

Optional X advertising

If you choose Allow X cookies, the browser game loads the X website pixel to build Mossvale website-visitor audiences and measure advertising on X. X receives visit and browser information, which can include the page URL, IP address, browser details and cookie identifiers, and may connect these visits to your X account. We do not add player names, account emails, wallet addresses, chat or gameplay records to the pixel. Automatic form matching and additional automatic events are disabled.

The tag stays off until you opt in. Declining does not affect play. It is not loaded in Mossvale’s native apps or on account, authentication-callback, wallet, support or privacy pages. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep the tag off.

Your choice is stored in this browser for this website. Use the controls below to change it at any time. Declining reloads other open, tagged Mossvale tabs on this website to stop the already-loaded tag; your game may briefly reconnect. Changing this preference does not remove information X already received or cookies stored by X. You can also use X’s personalized ads controls and your browser’s cookie controls. See X’s privacy policy.

Optional chat translation

When a player enables chat translation, the text of messages delivered to that player can be sent to Google Cloud Translation, including world, party and whisper messages. Mossvale sends the message text and target language without appending character names or account identifiers. Translations are temporarily cached in realm memory to reduce repeat requests; the game keeps original messages for reporting. The language preference is stored on the device. Google explains how translation data is processed.

Translations are automatic and may be inaccurate. See Google’s translation disclaimer; the original text remains available in chat.

Storage and security

Saved game data is held on the game servers. The browser stores preferences, local guest information where guest play is available, and temporary sign-in state; the account provider uses cookies for sign-in. Direct native wallet sign-in keeps temporary sign-in state in the app’s embedded browser while the wallet browser holds its own sign-in cookies. Older app builds may retain wallet connection keys and session state in device-only secure storage. Account access tokens used by these account pages stay in memory.

Operational copies and backups support recovery. Purchase, refund and security records have different purposes from your active game profile. This notice does not promise a fixed deletion period for those records. Contact support for details about a particular record or request.

Delete your account

Open Your account → Delete account from the website or the game’s Account menu. Sign in, verify your sign-in again, and type DELETE ACCOUNT to confirm. You can initiate the request without installing the mobile app.

Deletion removes the sign-in identity, all characters and their saved progress in both realms, social references to the account, and reports made by or about that account. Live access is closed. Country-statistics records are removed on the next successful statistics refresh after the deletion request; historical aggregate activity totals remain. This is permanent and is different from deleting one character or uninstalling the app.

A limited hashed account-deletion record remains to prevent old sign-in tokens from recreating the deleted account. Native purchase, receipt, refund and related event records retain their account hash and character identifier for payment verification, duplicate-payment prevention and recovery. Deletion does not automatically refund a purchase, erase a public blockchain transaction, or delete copies independently kept by another player.

If a MOSS order or auction reservation is still settling, the request is blocked before deletion begins. Resolve the payment, then retry. After a request is accepted, use Check deletion status on the account page. If you cannot sign in or confirm the outcome, email support.

Your choices and requests

You can manage the sign-in details offered by your provider through Your account, choose whether to connect a wallet, control in-game communication with Ignore and Report, and request full account deletion.

To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of information, or to raise a privacy concern, email support@mossvale.world. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Do not send a password, private key or recovery phrase.
