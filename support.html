A little help for your adventure

Contact Mossvale support for account, gameplay or purchase problems.

support@mossvale.world

Include your character name, realm, device, what happened, and roughly when. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, access tokens, wallet private keys, or recovery phrases.

Account & sign-in

Use Your account to manage sign-in settings, change a password, sign out, or start full account deletion. For a forgotten password, choose Sign in and use the password-reset option on the account service.

To switch characters or delete just one character, open character selection in the game. Full account deletion applies to all characters in both realms.

If you used a wallet to sign in, reconnect the same wallet. Wallet sign-in proves account ownership; it does not authorize a payment. Support will never ask for your wallet recovery phrase.

Purchase help

If you were charged but did not receive an item, avoid buying it again. Open the store on the original account and check the purchase status or recovery option. Some purchases need time to verify, and a reward conflict can require support.

For an App Store or Google Play purchase, include the store order reference from your receipt, the item name, and the affected character. Do not send payment-card details or an account sign-in token. Refund requests are handled through the platform where you paid; contact us if you need help with a missing reward.

For a MOSS payment made on the website, include the public transaction hash. A payment that is still settling may temporarily prevent account deletion. Public blockchain transactions cannot be undone by deleting a game account.

Player safety

Use Report on a player or chat message to send abuse to the game masters. Use Ignore to stop chat and invitations from that player. If the game is unavailable, email support with the character name, realm, time and relevant screenshot.

  • Treat other players with respect. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate or share sexual content.
  • Keep names and chat suitable for a general gaming community. Do not share private information, scams or spam.
  • Game masters review reports and may remove access for rule violations.

Connection problems

Check your connection, then use Reconnect or reload the page. During a server update, wait for the realm to return before trying again. Sign in with the same account to recover your saved roster.

If the problem continues, tell support whether you chose Europe or North America, your device and browser or app version, and any error shown.

Privacy requests

Read the privacy notice, or email support to ask about your information. To request full account deletion yourself, open Delete account.
