About this data

Players. Online counts connected sessions. Hourly activity counts each account once per UTC hour across realms. Registered users excludes service accounts.

MOSS. Auction volume and treasury payouts are all-time finalized totals. Auction volume excludes listings, cancellations, gold trades and store purchases. Treasury payouts include voucher and gold-round claims from current and older treasuries, excluding owner withdrawals. The vault balance may include funds reserved for vouchers and gold rounds.

Countries. Each account uses its latest connection country, not nationality. VPNs can affect location; Unknown means location is unavailable. All time uses the last 30 days.

History. Longer charts average the available readings. Dashed lines mark partial coverage. Hours without readings stay empty. Peaks are recorded observations. Counts refresh every 30 seconds.

Country data: MaxMind GeoLite2 and Cloudflare. No IP addresses are published.
